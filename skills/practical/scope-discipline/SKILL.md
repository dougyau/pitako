# Scope discipline

Apply this criterion in execute planning, implementation and its reviews. It does
not authorize changes or replace `verify-behavior`.

Before expanding work or verification, ask:

1. What authorized outcome needs this action?
2. Has a possibility, recommendation or interpretation become an obligation?
3. What concrete guarantee remains unfulfilled or undemonstrated if it is omitted?
4. Does the accumulated trajectory still solve the original assignment?

The latest brief alone does not justify an obligation. Trace inherited demands to
the authorized assignment, current human decisions and applicable limits. Agent
reports are evidence, not human authority. An old expansion does not become
legitimate by recurring in later briefs; a resolved historical demand is not
necessarily pending work. Follow relevant provenance, not an exhaustive history.

## Preserve necessary work

Identify only the unjustified expansion in a mixed task; preserve its necessary
work. Explicit requirements may need tests without a previous failure. Many tests,
high cost, an internal probe, uncertainty or repetition alone do not prove drift.
Detecting repetition does not authorize omitting an admitted gate. Do not add
optional improvements on your own or dispense with admitted tasks, requirements,
checks or gates. Necessary dependencies remain subject to existing scope controls.

Directors formulate their sole plan from authorized sources and original reports,
using independent observations as advice, not automatically inheriting questioned
expansions. Workers apply this criterion before expanding work or verification.
Review critique and synthesis examine the origin of demands before passing them
to another cycle; distinguish concrete unmet guarantees from optional improvements.
Compliance still independently evaluates whole-assignment sufficiency and evidence:
less unnecessary work does not establish a complete delivery.

## Advisory, not another gate

An independent observation identifies its source reference, demand and reason,
with a memo and evidence limits. It is not approval, rejection, certification or
an order to remove work. Empty observations are valid. Analytical uncertainty
alone creates no block, investigation, tests or extra human approval. Technical
child success proves neither the judgment nor the director's obedience.

No per-action inventory, extra report, exhaustive defense or ceremonial response
to every observation is required. This criterion adds no semantic admission veto,
review round, watchdog, budget extension or waiver. Preserve native Stop,
permissions, technical failures, persistence barriers, checkpoints, completion
sufficiency and existing gates.
